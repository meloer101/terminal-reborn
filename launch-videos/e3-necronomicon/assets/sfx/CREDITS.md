## Images, type and code

- `assets/brand/sky-wavi.png` — the sky-filled WAVI exported from the Wavi Figma file (page 「官网 · W5 重塑（噪音门面）」).
- `assets/brand/orbit.svg` — the Wavi mark from `site/src/assets/orbit.svg` (path copied into `assets/lib/orbit-path.js`).
- `assets/lib/tube.js` — film D1's seekable copy of the site's CRT (`site/src/scripts/crt.ts`), plus a `calm` uniform.
- Every engraving, eye, page, seal and the flesh of the binding is drawn procedurally (`assets/lib/plates.js`,
  `assets/lib/film.js`); no stock images, scans or third-party artwork.
- Three.js r158 (MIT) from jsDelivr renders the book.
- Fonts: UnifrakturMaguntia (SIL OFL 1.1, `assets/fonts/OFL-UnifrakturMaguntia.txt`) and Bodoni Moda roman and italic
  (SIL OFL 1.1, `assets/fonts/OFL-BodoniModa.txt`), subset with pyftsubset from Google Fonts; Archivo and JetBrains Mono
  subsets from the site.
- The couplet is from H. P. Lovecraft, "The Nameless City" (1921), public domain.
